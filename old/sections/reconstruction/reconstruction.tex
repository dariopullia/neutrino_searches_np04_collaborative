The comprehensive reconstruction conducted in this study employs the widely adopted DUNE software tools, which are integral to the LArSoft framework. The event reconstruction process is executed utilizing the Pandora multi-algorithm approach for automated pattern recognition. It includes algorithms to reconstruct the paths of particles from the raw electronic signals recorded by the detector, identifying tracks, showers, and other features. Customized settings were crafted to harmonize the standard ProtoDUNE reconstruction, which incorporates the presence of external backgrounds, with the Far Detector reconstruction, which effectively captures the search for energetic neutrinos in the beam direction.
